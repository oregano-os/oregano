# Sprint SOP

## Required resources

Read [references/operating-rules.md](references/operating-rules.md) before any
weekly or close decision. For Friday Close, use
[assets/friday-update-template.md](assets/friday-update-template.md) and
[assets/friday-completeness-template.md](assets/friday-completeness-template.md).
Do not copy either asset into another Blueprint location.

## Workflow

1. Load the validated Sprint declaration, current schedule version, authorized Company Records projections, and exact logical bindings.
2. Verify freshness and the current Sprint identity. Reconcile or block when the provider projection is stale or conflicting.
3. Use the frozen participant and work-item versions required by the current phase of the weekly process.
4. Apply only deterministic Sprint-domain outcomes for timing, absence exclusion, committed-task completeness, actual effort, and Rollover eligibility.
5. Render the resulting neutral intent for the configured provider and language. Never change its audience or factual category.
6. Execute an effect only through a resolved Tool, with its required authority, idempotency identity, and evidence.
7. Record the no-effect reason when any prerequisite is missing or ambiguous.

## Quality gates

- Every person-facing statement is grounded in a named event or record version.
- Every included participant appears once in a completeness report; excluded participants do not appear.
- Every committed task is represented once in a structurally complete close.
- Actual effort is never replaced by planned effort or an estimate.
- Ready never means prioritized or committed.
- The Agent never judges a person or invents a reason for missing or open work.
